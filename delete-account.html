Delete your account

You can delete your True Find account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open True Find and log in with your mobile number.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings (the gear button).
  3. Tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.
Open True Find

What is deleted

Your profile, photos, chats, coins, followers and Reconnect data.

We keep only what the law or safety needs: payment and withdrawal records (for tax), call records (without your name), and records of reports and bans, for as long as required. If you are a Creator or Host, please withdraw your earnings first.

Can't open the app?

Send us the mobile number (or email) of your account and we will delete it for you.
