Built for safety

18+ onlyTrue Find is only for adults. Under-18 accounts are closed.
Verified Creators and HostsMembers who earn on True Find are checked with a live selfie video first.
Your number stays privateYour mobile number is never shown to other members. Calls are never recorded by us.
Block and reportOn every profile, chat and call. Our team reviews every report.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code.
  2. Make your profile: name, photo, city and interests.
  3. Buy coins and use them for calls, gifts and games. See Pricing.
  4. Creators and Hosts earn from the calls and gifts they receive, and withdraw to their own UPI account.
