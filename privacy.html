Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · 25 NOVEMBER 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information True Find collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the rights you have over it. It covers the True Find Android app (including Secret Dating) and our website truefindapp.com. It follows India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules, and Google Play's User Data policy.

18 SECTIONS

On this page

True Find ("True Find", "we", "us") decides how and why your personal information is used, so under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the "Data Fiduciary" and you are the "Data Principal". The business name and address behind True Find, and how to reach our Grievance Officer, are shown at the end of this page and on our Contact page (truefindapp.com/contact).

This policy, our Terms of Use and our Community Guidelines are read together. Words like "Creator", "Host", "Expert", "coins" and "Secret Dating" mean the same here as in the Terms of Use.

When you create your account

  • Your mobile number. You sign in with a one-time code (OTP) sent to it on WhatsApp or SMS. Your number is your account: one number opens only one account. It is never shown to other members.
  • The email address of the Google account you use when your account is made.
  • Your name, gender and date of birth (we show your age, never the date), your state and city.

Your profile

  • Profile photos, bio, hobbies and interests, and if you choose to add them: occupation, height, weight and what kind of relationship you are looking for.
  • Your preferences for who you would like to meet (age range, gender, state and city).
  • Stories you post. A story disappears after 24 hours.

Verification (Creators, Hosts and Experts)

  • A live selfie photo and short selfie video taken in the app, to check that you are a real person and match your photos.
  • For payouts: the name and UPI ID of your own bank account, and where tax law needs it, your PAN and a photo of your PAN card.
  • For Experts: your application answers and the phone number you share for the interview call.

When you contact us

Messages, screenshots and other details you send to Help & support, to our Grievance Officer or by email.

  • Calls: who called whom, when, how long, the coins used, and the rating each person gave. We do not record the sound or the video of calls.
  • Secret Dating: likes, requests you send and receive, chats with their messages, photos and voice notes (up to 60 seconds), gifts, profile visits, follows and the plan or credits you have.
  • Live rooms and Live Dance: rooms you join or host, Super Chats, hearts, gifts, votes, chat messages in the room and reports.
  • Games: games played, who you played with, your answers inside the game (for example the mood you pick in Mood Changer) and coins used.
  • Expert Talk: bookings, call times and the feedback you give.
  • Wallet: coins bought and spent, plans, earnings, withdrawals, payout fees, tax cut (TDS) and payment references.
  • Safety records: reports you make or that are made about you, blocks, warnings and bans.
  • Ratings and a quality score worked out from ratings and call history, used to show reliable Creators and Hosts first.
  • A random device id kept by the app on your phone, your IP address, phone or browser type and app version. We use these to keep your account secure and to find fake or repeated accounts on the same device.
  • A notification token, so we can send the notifications you allow.
  • Which screens you open, time spent, and error reports, so we can fix problems and improve the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your phone's GPS (exact) location, your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, the list of apps on your phone, or your card number, UPI PIN or bank password.

We use only the state and city you write in your profile. The Secret Dating map places members at their city, never at an exact address. If you turn off "Show my location on profile" in Settings → Privacy, your city is hidden and you are taken off the map.

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can say no, or turn it off later in your phone's settings; only that feature stops working.

  • Microphone: voice and video calls, live audio rooms, Live Dance, voice games and voice notes in chat.
  • Camera: video calls, Live Dance, and the live selfie video for verification.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick yourself for your profile, stories or chat.
  • Notifications: to tell you about calls, messages, likes and offers.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show an incoming call on your lock screen, like a normal phone call.
  • Running in the background during a call: so the call keeps working when you switch to another app.

We use your personal information only with your consent, which you give when you create your account and when you allow a permission, or where the law allows it without consent (for example to follow a law, a court order, or in a medical emergency). We use it only for these purposes:

  • To sign you in and keep one account per mobile number.
  • To show your profile, and to connect calls, chats, live rooms, games, Expert Talk and Secret Dating.
  • To count coins and credits, bill calls, pay Creators, Hosts and Experts, cut tax where the law requires, and keep accounts.
  • To keep members safe: verify members, check photos, block phone numbers and links in Secret Dating chat, find fake or repeated accounts, review reports and stop fraud.
  • To send notifications you allow, including offers from True Find.
  • To answer support requests and complaints, and to improve and fix the app.
  • To follow the law and answer valid requests from courts and authorities.

We do not use your information for a new purpose without telling you and, where the law needs it, asking you first. We do not sell your information, we do not show third-party advertising, and we do not use your photos or conversations in our marketing without asking you.

To keep Secret Dating safe, photos sent in Secret Dating chat and photos posted as stories are checked automatically by an AI service before anyone else sees them. The check looks only for nudity, sexual or violent content, and phone numbers, links, QR codes or social media IDs on the photo. If the check fails, the photo is not sent or posted.

In the Mood Changer game, an AI service helps choose and word the questions from our approved list. The private reason behind your mood is never shown to the other player.

These checks only allow or stop a photo or pick a question. No decision about your account (such as a ban) is made only by an automatic system; our team reviews reports and decides.

  • Your name, age, gender, state and city, photos, bio, hobbies, stories, online or busy status, your badges (Verified, Creator, Host, Expert) and followers.
  • On Secret Dating, a member you visit may be told that someone viewed their profile. Your name and photo are shown only to members whose plan or verification allows it; for others the visit is shown blurred.
  • Messages, gifts and photos you send are seen by the person you send them to.

Other members never see your mobile number, email address, date of birth, verification photo or video, UPI or PAN details, or your wallet.

Voice calls, video calls, live rooms and Live Dance pass through Agora, our real-time voice and video provider. True Find does not record calls, lives or voice games, and does not use your voice to train AI.

Worth knowing

Calls and chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Another person could still record their own screen without telling you. Never share anything in a call or chat that you would not want shared, and report anyone who records or threatens you.

We share personal information only as described here, and only as much as each needs:

  • Supabase: our servers — database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Our AI service provider: the automatic checks described above.
  • GitHub: hosts our website, truefindapp.com.
  • Agora: voice and video for calls, live rooms, Live Dance and voice games.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) and our SMS provider: to deliver your one-time sign-in code.
  • Google: sign-in with Google, Firebase notifications, and Google Play for app downloads and in-app payments.
  • Stripe and other payment partners: to take payments made on our website. They handle your card or UPI details; we never receive them.
  • Other members, as described in "What other members can see".
  • Courts, police and government authorities, when the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to stop fraud.

Our service providers may only use your information to provide their service to us and must keep it safe. If True Find, or its business, is ever sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner, who must respect this policy; we will tell you before that happens.

Your information travels over secure (HTTPS) connections and is stored on our service providers' cloud servers, which may be in India or in other countries the Government of India allows. One-time codes are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, payment card details never reach us, and only team members who need it for their work can see personal information.

No system is perfectly safe. If a personal data breach happens, we will tell the affected members and the Data Protection Board of India, report cyber security incidents to CERT-In as the law requires, and act quickly to limit the harm.

The app and website keep small pieces of information on your phone or in your browser (local storage), such as your sign-in, settings and the random device id. This keeps you signed in and the app fast. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers from advertising companies. Clearing the app's data or your browser's storage removes them and signs you out.

We keep your information while your account is open. Stories disappear after 24 hours. When you delete your account in the app, your profile, photos, chats, likes, follows, stories and notification settings are deleted or made anonymous straight away. A deletion asked for on our website is done within 7 days after we confirm the account is yours.

The law requires us to keep a few things for a fixed time even after deletion. We keep only these, safely, and then delete them:

  • Your registration details, such as your mobile number: 180 days after deletion (IT Rules, 2021).
  • Records of content or accounts removed under the law: 180 days, or longer if a court or authority asks (IT Rules, 2021).
  • Logs of how personal information was processed: at least 1 year (Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025).
  • Payment, purchase, payout and tax records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Records of serious reports and bans: so a banned person cannot come back, and for safety investigations. Call records are kept without your name.
