Child Safety Standards

LAST UPDATED · 25 NOVEMBER 2026

True Find has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These standards apply to everyone who uses the True Find app and website.

6 SECTIONS

On this page

True Find is only for people aged 18 or older. Every member gives their date of birth when signing up, and accounts of anyone under 18 are closed.

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — pictures, videos, drawings, AI-made images or text that sexualise a child.
  • Grooming: contacting a child, or someone you believe is a child, for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual talk with, or asking for sexual content from, anyone under 18.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or offering or asking for a child for any sexual purpose.
  • Sharing, asking for or linking to such content in any part of True Find.
  • Date of birth checked at sign-up; under-18s cannot create an account.
  • Creators, Hosts and Experts are verified with a live selfie video before they can earn.
  • New profile photos are checked by our team before other members see them.
  • Phone numbers, links and social media IDs are blocked in Secret Dating chat.
  • Block and Report are on every profile, chat and call, and every report is reviewed.

When we find or are told about CSAE, we remove the content, permanently ban the accounts involved, keep the evidence and report it to the police and other authorities as Indian law requires (including the POCSO Act and the IT Act). We cooperate fully with law enforcement.

  • In the app: tap Report on the person's profile, in the chat or during the call.
  • By email to our child-safety contact (below).
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 112.
  • Childline India: call 1098.
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in (you can report child sexual abuse material without giving your name), or call 1930.

Our Grievance Officer (below) is also our child-safety contact person. They receive and act on child-safety reports and notices, including notices from Google Play.

Contact us · Grievance Officer

You can also write to us in the app: Profile → Settings → Help & support. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 7 days. Not satisfied? Appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee at gac.gov.in within 30 days.
