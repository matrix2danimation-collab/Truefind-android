Grievance Officer

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000, its rules and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, complaints about content, other members, safety or your personal data go to our Grievance Officer. They are also our child-safety contact.

We acknowledge every complaint within 24 hours and resolve it within 7 days. A complaint about a nude, sexual, fake (morphed) or AI-made picture of you, or someone pretending to be you, is acted on within 2 hours. Not satisfied with our decision? You can appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee at gac.gov.in within 30 days.
