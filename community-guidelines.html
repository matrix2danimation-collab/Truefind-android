Community Guidelines

LAST UPDATED · 26 NOVEMBER 2026

True Find is for real, respectful conversations between adults, in calls, live rooms, games, Expert Talk and Reconnect. These guidelines apply to everything you do on True Find, in public and in private. Read them with our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Breaking them can get your content removed and your account suspended or permanently banned.

Breaking these guidelines can also be a crime. Some of the laws that may apply (punishments are decided by the courts):

Harassment of women and girls

Sexual harassment, voyeurism (watching or recording someone's private acts), stalking, including online, and words or gestures meant to insult a woman's modesty are offences under sections 75, 77, 78 and 79 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS).

Obscene and sexual content

Publishing or sending obscene material online, or sexually explicit material, is punishable under sections 67 and 67A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act). Capturing or sharing images of someone's private parts without consent is an offence under section 66E of the IT Act.

Children

Any sexual content involving a child is punishable under section 67B of the IT Act and the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (POCSO), which also requires such cases to be reported to the police.

Threats and blackmail

Criminal intimidation (threats) is an offence under section 351 BNS, and extortion, including threatening to leak photos to get money, under section 308 BNS.

Fraud and impersonation

Cheating is an offence under section 318 BNS and cheating by pretending to be someone else under section 319 BNS. Using someone's identity or password online, and cheating by impersonation using a phone or computer, are offences under sections 66C and 66D of the IT Act.

Defamation and hate

Harming someone's reputation with false statements is defamation under section 356 BNS. Promoting enmity between groups on grounds of religion, race, caste, language or place is an offence under section 196 BNS.

Drugs, gambling and animals

Drugs are covered by the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, betting and gambling by central and state gambling laws, and cruelty to animals by the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960.

National honour

Insulting the National Flag, the Constitution or the National Anthem is an offence under the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971.

This list is for general information only and is not legal advice.
